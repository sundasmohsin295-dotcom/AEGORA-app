## 2. Source Trust Levels & Provenance Integrity
To ensure absolute integrity and prevent misinformation:
- **PRIMARY (98% Trust)**: Authoritative bodies (CISA, NIST, RFCs, direct vendor security bulletins).
- **HIGH_AUTHORITY (92% Trust)**: Established threat intelligence providers (MITRE, CERT/CC, Mandiant, CrowdStrike).
- **SECONDARY (78% Trust)**: Reputable reporting publications (SecurityWeek, BleepingComputer).
- **COMMUNITY (65% Trust)**: Community research, GitHub advisories, and social telemetry.
- **UNVERIFIED (40% Trust)**: Unconfirmed rumors, speculative zero-days.

Every card displays explicit badges (**CACHED SNAPSHOT**, **SIMULATED SCENARIO**, **LIVE FEED**) so learners never mistake educational simulations for live network connections.
