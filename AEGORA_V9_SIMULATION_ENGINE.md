# AEGORA v9.0 — CAREER SIMULATION UNIVERSE & WORKPLACE 2.0

## Overview
AEGORA v9.0 introduces 8 distinct simulated corporate archetypes and an interactive workplace inbox environment featuring branching consequence multiverse timelines.

---

## 1. Eight Simulated Corporate Archetypes
1. **Financial Services (Apex Global Banking)**: Wire fraud, credential stuffing, ransomware, PCI-DSS 4.0, SOX.
2. **Cloud SaaS (OmniCloud Enterprise)**: IAM STS privilege escalation, multi-tenancy bypass, SOC 2 Type II.
3. **Healthcare System (St. Jude Memorial Health)**: Medical IoT ransomware, EHR exfiltration, HIPAA, life safety.
4. **University Campus (Aegora State University)**: Decentralized BYOD, student credential phishing, FERPA.
5. **Government Agency (Federal Cyber Agency)**: Nation-state APTs, supply chain vulnerabilities, FedRAMP High.
6. **Industrial Manufacturing (Titan Heavy Industries)**: Modbus / SCADA manipulation, safety instrumented system bypass, IEC 62443.
7. **E-Commerce Platform (Zephyr Marketplace)**: Magecart web skimming, DDoS mitigation, GDPR / CCPA.
8. **AI Tech Startup (NovaStream AI Labs)**: LLM prompt injection, shadow SaaS, supply-chain dependency typosquatting.

## 2. Workplace Simulator 2.0 & Multiverse Consequence Engine
- Simulates realistic noise, false positives, executive pressure (urgent Slack messages from CFOs), and ambiguity.
- Every major incident decision forks into 3 parallel multiverse timelines:
  - **Learner Timeline**: Evaluates real decision impact (downtime hours, exfiltrated data, dollar cost).
  - **Premature / Hesitant Timeline**: Illustrates consequences of containment delay.
  - **Optimal Best-Practice Timeline**: Demonstrates gold-standard triage and communication.
