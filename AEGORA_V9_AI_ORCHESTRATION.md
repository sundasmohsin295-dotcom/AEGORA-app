# AEGORA v9.0 — AI ORCHESTRATION & TRUTH LAYER

## Overview
AEGORA v9.0 utilizes a multi-persona AI orchestration pipeline with strict epistemic certainty attribution (AEGORA Truth Layer) and cognitive evaluation arenas.

---

## 1. AEGORA Truth Layer (Certainty Attribution)
Every AI claim, feedback point, and evaluation explicitly states its epistemic foundation:
- **KNOWN**: Directly verified by RFC specification, deterministic telemetry, or signed proof.
- **INFERRED**: Derived from heuristic rule engines and pattern correlation.
- **ESTIMATED**: Probabilistic calibration based on student's historic performance.
- **GENERATED**: Synthesized dynamic scenario with sandbox validation.
- **UNKNOWN**: Explicit acknowledgment of incomplete telemetry or missing information.

---

## 2. Advanced Cognitive Arenas
- **Cyber Feynman Arena**: Evaluates the learner's ability to explain technical concepts to 4 distinct audiences (Beginner, Software Engineer, SOC Analyst, CISO) with automatic jargon detection and adaptation scoring.
- **Executive Incident Boardroom**: Evaluates plain-English crisis communication under C-Suite interrogation.
- **Confidence vs. Performance Calibration**: Measures whether learner confidence matches actual investigative accuracy, diagnosing overconfidence, underconfidence, or hesitation.
