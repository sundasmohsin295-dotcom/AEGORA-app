# AEGORA v8.0 Release Notes — Autonomous Cyber Learning & Career Ecosystem

## Major Innovations

### 1. Cyber Twin 2.0 & Evidence-Grounded Proof Trails
- Upgraded the central learner intelligence engine to track **8 core competency dimensions** with explicit explanations for *why* each score exists and verified evidence source trails.
- Integrated dynamic confidence calibration (`Well-Calibrated ±4%`, `Tending Overconfident`, `Tending Hesitant`).

### 2. Personal Cyber Mentor Engine (1 Primary + 3 Optional Actions)
- Generates "Today's Mission" answering "What should I do today?" with clear estimated durations, targeted skills, and pedagogical rationales.

### 3. Purple Team Arena: Signature "Self vs Self" Experience
- 5-Round Duel:
  - Round 1: Red Team Staging (select TTPs, stealth payloads)
  - Round 2: Telemetry Synthesis (compiles attack into Sysmon & Suricata telemetry)
  - Round 3: Blue Team Triage (investigate unlabeled telemetry)
  - Round 4: Incident Containment (execute host isolation & firewall drops)
  - Round 5: Forensic Debrief ("Would you have caught yourself?")

### 4. Real SOC Shift Simulator
- 30-minute high-fidelity shift simulation processing concurrent alerts, discriminating false positives (e.g. WSUS patch windows) from true malware intrusions (e.g. Mimikatz LSASS dumps).

### 5. Voice SOC Drills & Multiverse Replay
- Spoken dialogue simulation testing executive crisis communication without technical jargon.
- Counterfactual multiverse timeline branching demonstrating financial and infrastructure consequences of containment delays.

### 6. 5-Minute Skill Resurrection Engine
- Instant Ebbinghaus memory recovery drills for decaying competencies (e.g., DNS Tunneling TXT query analysis).

### 7. 18+ Infinite Career Families
- Shortest-path roadmap calculation mapping exact lab and evidence requirements for entry to senior roles.
