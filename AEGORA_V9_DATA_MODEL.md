# AEGORA v9.0 — DATA MODEL SPECIFICATION

## Overview
Comprehensive overview of the data structures in `CyberRealityV9Models.kt` powering the Cyber Reality Engine.

---

## 1. Primary Model Entities
- `ExternalIntelligenceCard`: Encapsulates real-world CVEs, CISA alerts, and threat bulletins with trust level and provenance markers.
- `EventToLessonTransformation`: Maps raw security events into 4-stage attack chains, defensive takeaways, and safe sandbox scenarios.
- `PersonalRadarItem`: Prioritized feed entity factoring in career goals, skill decay, and Mistake DNA.
- `KnowledgeNode30` & `KnowledgeEdge30`: 17 node types and 10 edge semantics representing the cybersecurity universe.
- `JobToTrainingSimulationResult`: Synthesized multi-tool practical lab extracted from raw job descriptions.
- `WorkplaceFeedItem` & `ConsequenceOutcome`: Simulated corporate messages and branching multiverse outcomes.
- `CyberFeynmanAssessment`: Multi-audience explanation scores (Accuracy, Clarity, Jargon, Adaptation).
- `PersonalKnowledgeVaultNote`: Learner notes bidirectionally linked to Knowledge Graph 3.0 nodes.
- `CompetitionLeaderboardEntry`: 4-tier skill band leaderboard with multi-dimensional MMR ratings.
