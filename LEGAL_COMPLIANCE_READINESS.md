## 2. Core Terms of Service Architecture

The AEGORA Terms of Service must enforce the following non-negotiable clauses:
1. **Authorized Educational Use Only**:
   - Learners explicitly agree never to use tools, scripts, payloads, or techniques learned on AEGORA against computer systems, networks, or applications without express, verifiable, written authorization from the system owner.
2. **Simulated Sandboxes**:
   - All PCAPs, Sysmon logs, malware samples, and network endpoints within AEGORA are simulated virtual environments designed strictly for defensive analysis and ethical vulnerability triage.
3. **No Employment Guarantee**:
   - AEGORA's job readiness scores, skill genomes, and career pathways are evidence-based educational metrics and do not constitute an explicit guarantee of job placement, hiring, or third-party certification passing.
4. **AI-Assisted Educational Content**:
   - AI mentor hints and dynamic scenario mutations are generated by machine learning models and provided "as-is" for training purposes.
